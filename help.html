Frequently asked questions

How do I download a resource?

Open a resource list and tap the download arrow. You will see the file size before the download starts, so you can decide whether to use data now or wait for Wi-Fi.

Why am I seeing a download limit?

Free accounts include a monthly download allowance. The library will show your remaining downloads before you start another file.

How do I save a paper for later?

Tap the heart beside any paper, marking key or note. It will appear on your Saved page, making it easier to return to next time.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

On the sign-in screen, choose “Forgot password?” and enter your account email. We will send a secure reset link to that inbox.

Can teachers share useful notes?

Yes. Contact the ZAFAR team with a clear, original resource. We check every contribution before adding it to the library.

Having connection trouble?

Try moving to a stronger signal, then refresh the page. If you are offline, previously opened material may still be available on your phone.
